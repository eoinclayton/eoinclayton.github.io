This blog is where I write about software, systems, and the corners of both that
most documentation quietly avoids.

I'm Eoin Clayton, a software engineer based in Canberra, Australia. I used to write a blog
called *Technicalities* — same general territory, different decade.

Posts here tend to be short. Something caught my attention, I looked into it
further than was strictly necessary, and I wrote it down.
